Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy describes how Parakeet Creative LLC ("Parakeet Creative", "we") handles information in our apps and games, including One More Cast.

The short version

Our games are designed to respect your privacy. We do not sell your data, we do not run third-party advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device. If you use Apple Game Center, your achievements and leaderboard scores are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Information we receive

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use it only to respond to you and to improve our products. We do not share it with anyone else.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment information.

Children

Our products are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and revise the date above.
